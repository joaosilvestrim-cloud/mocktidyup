The Goal

Build an integrated ERP-style hub that connects operations, people, customers and finance — giving TidyUp! visibility, efficiency and data-driven decisions, so growth funds better pay instead of outrunning the numbers. The reporting layer ships first; every later module plugs into the same hub, so nothing built now is lost.
